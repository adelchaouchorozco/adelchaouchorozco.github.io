Research

My research investigates lexical-semantic processes and their implications for broader cognition, while considering the impact of cross-cultural and cross-linguistic factors. I approach these questions by combining computational methods with behavioral experiments.

Research Areas

Semantic Memory in Bilinguals

How is meaning represented in the bilingual mind? My work has examined whether translation equivalents are truly equivalent, revealing that factors like concreteness modulate cross-linguistic connections. I've also studied how L2 immersion reshapes the links between translation equivalents over time.

Network Science & the Mental Lexicon

Using tools from network science, I model the structure of semantic memory. Recent work shows that network topology can reveal early signs of L1 lexical attrition in L2-dominant bilinguals, offering new methods for understanding language change.

Semantic Memory & Verbal Creativity

An emerging line of research explores how the organization of semantic memory relates to creative language use. How do people with different semantic network structures differ in their creative output?

Emotion Concepts Across Cultures

Emotion words don't translate perfectly across languages. I investigate how emotion concepts vary cross-culturally and what this tells us about the relationship between language, thought, and affective experience.

L2/L3 Acquisition & Processing

I study how additional languages are acquired and processed, including cross-linguistic transfer in third language acquisition and how proficiency assessments (like LexTALE) relate to broader language competence.

Computational Approaches to Language

I use computational tools including large language models, semantic similarity measures, and network analysis to complement traditional behavioral methods and gain new insights into language processing.

Methods

My research employs a variety of methods to investigate language and cognition:

Behavioral Experiments

Reaction time studies, priming paradigms, and production tasks to measure real-time language processing.

Network Science

Graph-theoretic analysis of semantic networks derived from word association data and distributional semantics.

Computational Modeling

Large language models and distributional semantic models to quantify meaning similarity and predict behavior.
