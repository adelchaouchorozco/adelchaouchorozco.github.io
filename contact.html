Opportunities

I am always interested in hearing from motivated students and researchers who want to explore questions at the intersection of language, cognition, and culture.

Prospective PhD Students

I welcome applications from students interested in pursuing doctoral research in psycholinguistics, bilingualism, or related areas. Strong candidates will have:

  • Background in linguistics, psychology, or cognitive science
  • Interest in experimental and/or computational methods
  • Strong quantitative and analytical skills
  • Excellent written and oral communication

Please email me with your CV and a brief description of your research interests.

Research Collaboration

I am open to collaboration on projects related to:

  • Bilingual language processing
  • Semantic memory and network science
  • Cross-cultural studies of language and emotion
  • Computational approaches to psycholinguistics

If you have an idea for collaboration, please reach out via email.
