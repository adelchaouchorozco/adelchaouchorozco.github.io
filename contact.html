Prospective research students

The Department of Linguistics and Translation offers MPhil and PhD research degrees. I welcome applicants interested in:

  • Bi-/Multilingualism
  • Lexical-semantic representation and processing
  • Cognitive control in language processing
  • Semantic networks
  • Language, cognition, and emotion
  • AI in education (teaching and learning)
  • AI and cognition
  • Cross-cultural studies
