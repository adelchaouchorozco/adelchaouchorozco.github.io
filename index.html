Adel Chaouch-Orozco

Assistant Professor · City University of Hong Kong

Psycholinguist studying how we represent and process meaning. My research explores lexical-semantic processes in monolinguals and bilinguals, combining computational methods with behavioral experiments to understand semantic memory, creativity, and emotion concepts across cultures.
