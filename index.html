Adel Chaouch-Orozco

Assistant Professor, City University of Hong Kong

I’m a psycholinguist interested in how we represent and process meaning. My research centres on lexical-semantic processing in monolinguals and bi-/multilinguals—how meaning is stored, retrieved, and shaped by culture, cognition, and affect. I combine computational methods, network science, and psycholinguistic experiments, and I also study second language acquisition and tools for language proficiency assessment. I hold a PhD in Psycholinguistics from the University of Reading.
