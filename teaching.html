Teaching

I teach in the Department of Linguistics and Translation at City University of Hong Kong, across the department’s undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. My teaching draws directly on my research in psycholinguistics, bilingualism, and the cognitive science of language.

Research Supervision

I welcome MPhil and PhD applicants interested in psycholinguistics, bilingualism, semantic memory, network-science approaches to the mental lexicon, and the relationship between language, emotion, and culture. Research degrees are offered through the department’s MPhil/PhD programme.

If you are considering applying, email me with a CV and a short statement of your research interests.

Thinking of applying? You can start by exploring the questions we are working on.

The strongest proposals build a meaningful connection with the lab’s research while bringing your own perspective and direction. Explore the Research Areas.
