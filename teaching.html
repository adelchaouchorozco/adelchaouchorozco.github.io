Teaching

I teach courses in linguistics, psycholinguistics, and research methods at City University of Hong Kong. My teaching philosophy emphasizes connecting theoretical concepts to real-world applications and developing critical thinking skills through hands-on research experience.

Current Courses

Courses taught at City University of Hong Kong:

Department of Linguistics and Translation

Psycholinguistics

An introduction to the cognitive processes underlying language comprehension and production. Topics include word recognition, sentence processing, speech perception, and the bilingual mind.

Department of Linguistics and Translation

Research Methods in Linguistics

A practical course covering experimental design, data collection, and statistical analysis for linguistic research. Students learn to design and conduct their own experiments.

Department of Linguistics and Translation

Bilingualism and Multilingualism

Examines the cognitive, social, and linguistic aspects of knowing multiple languages. Topics include language acquisition, code-switching, and the bilingual brain.

Teaching Philosophy

I believe in creating an engaging learning environment where students actively participate in the discovery process. My courses integrate current research findings with practical applications, helping students see how theoretical concepts apply to real linguistic phenomena.

I encourage students to develop their own research questions and provide mentorship for independent research projects. Several of my students have gone on to present their work at conferences and pursue graduate studies in linguistics and cognitive science.

Student Supervision

I supervise undergraduate and postgraduate research projects on topics related to:

  • Bilingual language processing
  • Lexical-semantic organization
  • Cross-cultural differences in language and cognition
  • Second/third language acquisition
  • Computational approaches to linguistic analysis
